🔢2048 — the number-merging puzzle

Goal: Slide all tiles in one direction; two equal tiles that collide merge into their double. Build your way up to a 2048 tile. The game ends when…

PuzzleHard5–15 min

Goal: Slide all tiles in one direction; two equal tiles that collide merge into their double. Build your way up to a 2048 tile. The game ends when the board is full and no two neighbours match.

Controls: Arrow keys or WASD on desktop; the direction buttons under the board on mobile.

Strategy: Pick one corner — most players use the bottom-left — and keep your largest tile locked there. Use only three of the four directions so the big tile never floats away, and build a descending "snake" of tiles next to it so merges chain in order.
