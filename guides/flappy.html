🐦Flappy Jump — one-tap timing

Goal: Flap through the gaps in the green pipes. Each gap you clear scores one point; touching a pipe or the ground ends the run.

ArcadeHard1–3 min

Goal: Flap through the gaps in the green pipes. Each gap you clear scores one point; touching a pipe or the ground ends the run.

Controls: Click the board, tap the Start / Flap button, or press Space — every tap is one flap.

Strategy: Fly in a rhythm, not in reactions. Let the bird sink to the lower third of the next gap, then give one firm flap. Two quick panicked taps almost always push you into the top pipe, so after each flap, wait and watch before tapping again.
