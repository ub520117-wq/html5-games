🐍Snake — the original arcade classic

Goal: Eat the red food squares to grow longer and score 10 points each. The game ends if you hit a wall or bite your own tail.

ArcadeEasy2–5 min

Goal: Eat the red food squares to grow longer and score 10 points each. The game ends if you hit a wall or bite your own tail.

Controls: Arrow keys or WASD on desktop; the on-screen direction pad on mobile. The snake cannot turn 180° — plan your turns one step ahead.

Strategy: Early on, roam the centre where you have escape routes on every side. As you grow, travel in wide loops along the edges and never chase food into a corner your own body is closing off. The tail end is always moving, so a gap that looks blocked often opens a second later.
