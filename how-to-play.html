How to Play

Controls for desktop and mobile, and tips to score higher.

On Desktop

Click any Play Now button. The game loads in the player above the list. Most games use arrow keys, WASD, Space, or your mouse. The exact controls are written under the player for every game. Use Restart to start a fresh round and Pause where available.

On Mobile

Tap a game, then use touch, swipe, or the on-screen buttons. Snake and Flappy include mobile controls, Breakout follows your finger, and tap-based games like Memory Match and Tic Tac Toe work with a single tap. No app install is needed.

Why HTML5 Games?

HTML5 games run directly in a modern browser using Canvas and JavaScript. That means no Flash, no plugins, no downloads, and the same game works on Windows, Mac, Android and iPhone. They also load fast on slow connections because everything is lightweight.

Tips to Score Higher

In Snake, stay near the centre early and avoid trapping yourself. In 2048, keep your biggest tile in one corner. In Memory Match, flip cards in a pattern instead of randomly. In Breakout, aim for the edges to create sharper rebound angles.
