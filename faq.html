Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions players ask most.

Are these games really free?

Yes. Every game on this page is free to play in your browser. There is no sign-up, no download and no in-game payment required to start playing.

Do I need to install anything?

No. These are HTML5 games, so they run inside Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari and other modern browsers. Just open a game and press Play.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. The site and games are responsive. Tap-based games work with touch, Snake includes on-screen direction buttons, and Breakout and Flappy respond to taps and swipes.

Why did my game stop when I switched games?

Each game runs on its own page. When you open another game, the previous one stops — that keeps every page fast. Your score is shown on the game screen while you play, so press Restart in the new game to begin.

Which game is best for a quick 2-minute break?

Flappy Jump, Snake and Tic Tac Toe are the fastest to start. If you want a longer session, try 2048, Minesweeper or Breakout.

Do you collect my personal data?

This static games page does not require an account and does not ask for personal information to play. If advertising is enabled, ad partners may use cookies as described in the Privacy Policy below.

Are the games safe for children?

Yes. The games contain no chat, no purchases, no gambling and no user-generated content, and they ask for no personal information. Advertising, when enabled, follows Google's policies; parents can manage personalised ads through Google Ads Settings, as explained in the Privacy Policy.

Will there be ads on this site?

Not yet — the site currently shows no ads. If advertising is added later to help keep the games free, you will be able to opt out of personalised advertising at any time via Google Ads Settings (www.google.com/settings/ads) or www.aboutads.info — you would still see ads, but they would not be based on your browsing history.

A game is not working. What should I do?

First refresh the page and make sure your browser is up to date — the games run in current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. If it still fails, send the game name, your device and your browser through the Contact section and it will be investigated.

Can I suggest a new game or report a wrong tip in a guide?

Yes — suggestions and corrections are welcome through the Contact section. The guides on this page are original to this site and are corrected whenever a game's rules or controls change.
