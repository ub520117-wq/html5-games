Privacy Policy

What this site collects, how cookies and advertising work here, and your rights.

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information HTML5 Games ("we", "this site") collects when you play the free browser games on this page, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using this site you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

This site has no accounts, no sign-up forms and no payment processing. We do not ask for your name, password, address or payment details in order to play. Game scores are shown on your screen during play only and are not sent to us. If you email us through the Contact section, we receive the email address and message you choose to send, and we use them only to reply to you.

Cookies and advertising

This site is supported by advertising provided by Google AdSense. In connection with those ads:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • Third-party ad servers or ad networks may use technologies such as cookies, JavaScript or web beacons in their advertisements, and may receive your IP address when this happens. This site has no control over cookies used by third-party advertisers; please consult the respective privacy policies of those ad servers for more detail.
  • If third-party ad serving has not been opted out of, the cookies of other third-party vendors or ad networks may also be used to serve ads on this site.

Third parties may be placing and reading cookies on your browser, or using web beacons to collect information, as a result of ad serving on this website. For more detail on how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Data collection, sharing and use by Google products

When advertising or other Google services are active on this site, Google and its partners may collect, share and use data — including cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, device identifiers and other identifiers, device-specific information and location information — as described in Google's own policies linked above. This data is used to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads where you have not opted out, and to prevent fraud and abuse. You can manage cookies at any time in your browser settings (blocking or deleting cookies may affect how ads and some site features work), and you can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where consent is required for personalised advertising (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), a consent message provided through a Google-certified consent management platform will be shown so you can accept or reject personalised ads before they are served. If you reject, you may still see non-personalised ads.

What this site itself stores

The games keep your score on screen in your browser's memory only. This site does not use accounts, does not run its own analytics database, and does not store your gameplay on any server. The only personal information we ever hold is an email you choose to send us, as described above.

Log files

Like most websites, hosting providers may record standard log information (IP address, browser type, Internet Service Provider, date and time, and pages visited) for security and traffic analysis. This information is not linked to anything personally identifiable on this site.

Children's privacy

The games on this site are suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, and to ask for its correction or deletion. Because we hold almost none — only emails you send us — such requests are simple: write to us from the Contact section and we will act on them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the site grows. The "Last updated" date at the top of this section will change when we do, and the current version always applies.

Consent

By using this website, you consent to this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
