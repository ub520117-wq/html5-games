About Reversi Mini

Outflank and flip discs on a 6×6 board. Most discs at the end wins. Reversi Mini is a free classic game you can play right here in your browser — no download and no sign-up needed. A typical session lasts around 4–8 min, and the difficulty is hard, so it is a good fit whether you have two minutes or a full coffee break. Your score stays on the game screen while you play, so press Restart any time and try to beat your last run.

How to Play Reversi Mini

Tap a square that outflanks white discs to flip them. The CPU plays white. Most discs when no moves remain wins.

Tips to Score Higher

  • Tip 1: Corners are gold: once you own a corner it can never be flipped.
  • Tip 2: Avoid giving your opponent the squares next to empty corners — they lead straight to them.
  • Tip 3: This is one of the tougher games on the site. Expect a few failed runs while you learn — short, focused attempts work better than long frustrated ones.
