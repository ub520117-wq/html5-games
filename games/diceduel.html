About Dice Duel

Roll against the computer — the higher roll banks its points. First to 30. Dice Duel is a free classic game you can play right here in your browser — no download and no sign-up needed. A typical session lasts around 2–4 min, and the difficulty is easy, so it is a good fit whether you have two minutes or a full coffee break. Your score stays on the game screen while you play, so press Restart any time and try to beat your last run.

How to Play Dice Duel

Press Roll. Higher roll scores its own value. First to 30 points wins.

Tips to Score Higher

  • Tip 1: There is no skill in a dice roll — enjoy the luck and don't chase a losing streak.
  • Tip 2: Set yourself a target score before you start, and stop when you reach it.
  • Tip 3: This is a gentle game — a good first pick if you are new here. Relax, learn the controls, and chase your own best score.
