About Duck Hunt

Ducks flash across the range — tap them before they escape. Duck Hunt is a free action game you can play right here in your browser — no download and no sign-up needed. A typical session lasts around 1 min, and the difficulty is medium, so it is a good fit whether you have two minutes or a full coffee break. Your score stays on the game screen while you play, so press Restart any time and try to beat your last run.

How to Play Duck Hunt

Click each duck quickly. Faster ducks later in the round.

Tips to Score Higher

  • Tip 1: Stay loose and watch the whole board — the next target can appear anywhere.
  • Tip 2: Short, quick taps beat big sweeping movements. Keep your cursor or finger near the centre.
  • Tip 3: This one rewards a little practice. Play two or three short rounds to learn the rhythm before chasing a high score.
