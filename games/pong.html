About Pong Classic

The original video game. Move your paddle, beat the computer — first to 7 wins. Pong Classic is a free arcade game you can play right here in your browser — no download and no sign-up needed. A typical session lasts around 3–6 min, and the difficulty is medium, so it is a good fit whether you have two minutes or a full coffee break. Your score stays on the game screen while you play, so press Restart any time and try to beat your last run.

How to Play Pong Classic

Move mouse / finger, or ↑ ↓ keys, to control the left paddle. First to 7 points wins.

Tips to Score Higher

  • Tip 1: Hit the ball with the edge of your paddle to send it at a sharper angle.
  • Tip 2: Stay near the centre of your side so you can reach shots in both directions.
  • Tip 3: This one rewards a little practice. Play two or three short rounds to learn the rhythm before chasing a high score.
