About Geography Scramble

Countries and places, all scrambled up. Geography Scramble is a free word game you can play right here in your browser — no download and no sign-up needed. A typical session lasts around 3–5 min, and the difficulty is medium, so it is a good fit whether you have two minutes or a full coffee break. Your score stays on the game screen while you play, so press Restart any time and try to beat your last run.

How to Play Geography Scramble

Unscramble each place name and type it, then press Check.

Tips to Score Higher

  • Tip 1: Look for common letter pairs first — TH, ER, IN and SH unlock most words.
  • Tip 2: Say the letters out loud; hearing them in a different order often reveals the word.
  • Tip 3: This one rewards a little practice. Play two or three short rounds to learn the rhythm before chasing a high score.
