About Color Stroop Test

The word says one colour, the ink says another. Tap the INK colour. Color Stroop Test is a free brain game you can play right here in your browser — no download and no sign-up needed. A typical session lasts around 1 min, and the difficulty is hard, so it is a good fit whether you have two minutes or a full coffee break. Your score stays on the game screen while you play, so press Restart any time and try to beat your last run.

How to Play Color Stroop Test

Ignore what the word says — tap the button matching the colour of the ink. 30 seconds.

Tips to Score Higher

  • Tip 1: Read the word, not the colour — then deliberately slow down and check yourself.
  • Tip 2: If you feel yourself speeding up, pause for one beat; rushing is what causes the mistakes.
  • Tip 3: This is one of the tougher games on the site. Expect a few failed runs while you learn — short, focused attempts work better than long frustrated ones.
