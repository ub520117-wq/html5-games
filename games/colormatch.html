About Word Color Match

The reverse Stroop: tap the colour the WORD names, not the ink. Word Color Match is a free brain game you can play right here in your browser — no download and no sign-up needed. A typical session lasts around 1 min, and the difficulty is hard, so it is a good fit whether you have two minutes or a full coffee break. Your score stays on the game screen while you play, so press Restart any time and try to beat your last run.

How to Play Word Color Match

Tap the button matching what the word SAYS, ignoring its ink colour. 30 seconds.

Tips to Score Higher

  • Tip 1: Read the word, not the colour — then deliberately slow down and check yourself.
  • Tip 2: If you feel yourself speeding up, pause for one beat; rushing is what causes the mistakes.
  • Tip 3: This is one of the tougher games on the site. Expect a few failed runs while you learn — short, focused attempts work better than long frustrated ones.
