About Boxing Reflex

A reaction test with a boxer twist — strike the pad the instant it flashes. Boxing Reflex is a free sports game you can play right here in your browser — no download and no sign-up needed. A typical session lasts around 1–2 min, and the difficulty is easy, so it is a good fit whether you have two minutes or a full coffee break. Tips to Score Higher

  • Tip 1: Ignore the decoys: wait for the real signal before you move, or you will lose more time than you save.
  • Tip 2: A steady rhythm beats frantic tapping. Breathe, watch, then act.
  • Tip 3: This is a gentle game — a good first pick if you are new here. Relax, learn the controls, and chase your own best score.
