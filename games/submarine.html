About Submarine Dive

Dodge mines and rocks on the ocean floor in your little submarine. Submarine Dive is a free action game you can play right here in your browser — no download and no sign-up needed. A typical session lasts around 2–5 min, and the difficulty is medium, so it is a good fit whether you have two minutes or a full coffee break. Your score stays on the game screen while you play, so press Restart any time and try to beat your last run.

How to Play Submarine Dive

Arrow keys or buttons move in all directions. Avoid the mines.

Tips to Score Higher

  • Tip 1: Stay near the middle of the play area so you always have an escape route on both sides.
  • Tip 2: Look ahead of your character, not at it — the danger is always coming from in front.
  • Tip 3: This one rewards a little practice. Play two or three short rounds to learn the rhythm before chasing a high score.
