About Hangman: Countries

Guess the country before your lives run out. Hangman: Countries is a free word game you can play right here in your browser — no download and no sign-up needed. A typical session lasts around 2–4 min, and the difficulty is hard, so it is a good fit whether you have two minutes or a full coffee break. Your score stays on the game screen while you play, so press Restart any time and try to beat your last run.

How to Play Hangman: Countries

Tap letters to guess the hidden country. Wrong letters cost a life — you have 6.

Tips to Score Higher

  • Tip 1: Start with the most common letters — E, A, R, I, O, T, N, S — before guessing rarer ones.
  • Tip 2: Use the word's length and any revealed letters to think of the shape of the word, not just single letters.
  • Tip 3: This is one of the tougher games on the site. Expect a few failed runs while you learn — short, focused attempts work better than long frustrated ones.
