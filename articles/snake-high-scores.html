Snake: how high scores are really built

SnakeStrategy

Beginners chase the food; experienced players manage space. The real skill in Snake is keeping an escape route that is always at least as long as your body needs to turn around. A reliable method is the wall-follow: pick a wall and travel along it in a slow spiral toward the centre, so your body packs into neat rows instead of scattered loops. Cut across the middle only for food that is clearly reachable, and always enter open space from the side nearest your tail — the tail is retreating, so that side keeps opening up. The classic beginner mistake is the celebration dash: grabbing three foods quickly in open space, then realising the only way out is through a gap your own neck is about to close. If a food looks risky, skip it. Food respawns; your run does not.
