Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers. See all 10 questions →

Are these games really free?

Yes. Every game on this page is free to play in your browser. There is no sign-up, no download and no in-game payment required to start playing.

Do I need to install anything?

No. These are HTML5 games, so they run inside Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari and other modern browsers. Just open a game and press Play.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. The site and games are responsive. Tap-based games work with touch, Snake includes on-screen direction buttons, and Breakout and Flappy respond to taps and swipes.

Why did my game stop when I switched games?

Each game runs on its own page. When you open another game, the previous one stops — that keeps every page fast. Your score is shown on the game screen while you play, so press Restart in the new game to begin.
